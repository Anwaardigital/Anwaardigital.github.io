Complete Guide to Meta Tags

Meta tags are the invisible labels that tell Google and social networks what your page is about. The title tag (around 50–60 characters) is the clickable headline in search results, and the meta description (around 150–160 characters) is the summary beneath it. Neither directly boosts rankings, but a clear, honest pair earns more clicks — and clicks are what bring visitors.

Open Graph tags (og:title, og:description, og:image) control how your link looks when shared on Facebook, LinkedIn and WhatsApp. Without them, platforms guess — often badly. Twitter Card tags do the same for X. Add a canonical URL when the same content appears at multiple addresses so search engines credit one version.
