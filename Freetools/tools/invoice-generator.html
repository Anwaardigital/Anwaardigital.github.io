Complete Guide to Invoicing

A good invoice gets you paid faster: it states who is billing, who owes, what was delivered, and exactly how much is due. Always include an invoice number (for your records and your client's), a date, an itemized list with quantities and prices, and any tax or discount shown separately so the total is easy to verify. Vague invoices get questioned; itemized ones get paid.

Freelancers in the US, UK and EU use the same basics, with local differences: EU invoices often need a VAT number and VAT rate, while US invoices usually show sales tax only where it applies. This generator keeps your data on your device — nothing is stored online — and produces a clean invoice you can print, save as PDF, or email as HTML.
