🛡️ About CyberSec Hub

CyberSec Hub is an educational project by Anwaar Digital with one mission: teach cybersecurity the ethical, legal way — free for everyone.

What We Teach

  • Cybersecurity basics — how to protect yourself, your family, and your devices.
  • Ethical hacking — penetration testing methodology and mindset, always with authorization.
  • Security tools — hands-on guides for your own lab and systems.
  • Careers — roadmaps, certifications, and honest advice for entering the field.

Our Ethics

Every guide on this site follows one rule: only test systems you own or have written permission to test. We teach defense, not attack. Unauthorized hacking is illegal in Pakistan and worldwide — and this site will never help anyone break that law.
