Privacy Policy — SafeBox

Developer: Malik Anwaar
App package: com.malakanwaar.safebox
Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SafeBox ("the App"), developed by Malik Anwaar, handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

  • SafeBox is 100% local. Your hidden photos, videos, and files never leave your device.
  • We do not upload, transmit, back up, or share your private files with anyone — including the developer.
  • Account information (only if you sign in): SafeBox offers optional Google sign-in via Firebase Authentication for login and PIN recovery. When you sign in, Google provides the App with your basic profile information (name, email address, and profile photo). This is used only for your login — never for your vault content.
  • There is no cloud sync of vault content, no analytics tracking, and no advertising SDK in SafeBox.

Permissions

  • SafeBox may request storage permission only to hide/unhide the files you select.
  • Your files are encrypted and stored locally; nothing is transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Services

SafeBox uses Firebase Authentication (Google) only for account login and PIN recovery. No other third-party SDKs — no advertising, no analytics, no cloud storage.

Internet use (login only): the App connects to the internet only for Google sign-in via Firebase Authentication (account login and PIN recovery via your Gmail). Your hidden photos, videos, and files never leave your device — the vault itself is 100% local.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Data Security

Your files are protected by your PIN and stored encrypted on your device. If you forget your PIN, use the Gmail recovery option you set up — we cannot recover your files for you because we never have access to them.

Your Rights

  • Because the App stores your content locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.
  • For account-based features, you may request deletion of your account data by emailing us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at malikanwaarapps@gmail.com.
