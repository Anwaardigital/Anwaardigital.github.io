Privacy Policy — Urdu Notes

Developer: Malik Anwaar
App package: com.malakanwaar.urdunotes
Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Urdu Notes ("the App"), developed by Malik Anwaar, handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

  • The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.
  • Urdu Notes works fully offline: your notes, categories, colors, reminders, and PIN are stored only on your device and never leave it.
  • The App integrates no Firebase, analytics, advertising, or cloud services of any kind.

Permissions

  • The App may request Android permissions (such as storage, camera, or microphone) only to perform the features you explicitly use.
  • Permissions are never used for background data collection.
  • You can revoke any permission at any time in your device Settings; the related feature will simply stop working.

Third-Party Services

Urdu Notes uses no third-party services, SDKs, or cloud backends. There is no account system, no login, and no data synchronization — everything stays on your device.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information on your device and, where applicable, in transit to Firebase. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Your Rights

  • Because the App stores your content locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.
  • For account-based features, you may request deletion of your account data by emailing us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
