Privacy Policy — Anwaar AI

Developer: Malik Anwaar
App package: com.malakanwaar.ai
Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Anwaar AI ("the App"), developed by Malik Anwaar, handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

  • When you chat with the AI, your messages are sent to a free third-party AI text API solely to generate a reply.
  • We do not store your conversations on our servers, and we do not link them to your identity.
  • The App does not collect your name, email, or other personal identifiers.

Permissions

  • The App may request microphone permission only if you use voice input. Voice is converted to text on your device or by the system speech service.

Third-Party Services

Chat messages are processed by a free third-party AI text API to generate replies. The API provider's privacy policy applies to that transient processing.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information on your device and, where applicable, in transit to Firebase. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Your Rights

  • Because the App stores your content locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.
  • For account-based features, you may request deletion of your account data by emailing us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us
