Privacy Policy — Quran Teacher 786

Developer: Malik Anwaar
App package: com.malakanwaar.quranteacher786
Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quran Teacher 786 ("the App"), developed by Malik Anwaar, collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you register or log in, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored securely via Firebase Authentication). Your selected role (student, teacher, or admin) and profile details are stored in our database.
  • Teacher booking information: When you book a class, we store the booking details (teacher, date, time, and status) so you and the teacher can see upcoming and past bookings.
  • Chat messages: Messages you send to teachers or support through the in-app chat are stored so the conversation history is available to both sides.
  • Learning data: Homework assigned, progress reports, and reminders you create are stored to provide the App's learning features.
  • Teacher registration documents: If you apply as a teacher, the profile photo, ID document, and certificates you upload are transmitted to our image hosting provider (Cloudinary) for review.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and log you in securely.
  • To operate teacher search, booking, chat, homework, and progress features.
  • To send you class and prayer-time reminders you requested (local notifications on your device).
  • To review teacher applications.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for advertising.

Payments

The App does not collect any payment information. There is no in-app payment flow — booking a teacher is free inside the App.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google): We use Firebase Authentication for secure login/registration and Firebase Realtime Database to store your account, bookings, chat, and learning data. Data is hosted by Google under Google's privacy standards.
  • Cloudinary: Teacher registration photos and documents are uploaded to Cloudinary for storage and review.

The App does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: required — login, booking, chat, and all cloud features need an internet connection.
  • Notifications / exact alarms / boot: used only for the class and prayer reminders you set. You can disable them in your device Settings.

Permissions are never used for background data collection unrelated to the features above.

Data Security

Account passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never stored in plain text by us. Database access is restricted so each user can only access their own data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Data Retention and Your Rights

  • Your account data is kept while your account is active.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us at the address below.
  • You can also clear the App's local data or uninstall the App at any time.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. Accounts are created by parents/guardians or adult users. If you believe a child has provided us personal data without parental consent, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request data deletion, contact us at malikanwaarapps@gmail.com.
