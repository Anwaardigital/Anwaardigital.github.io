🌙 Who We Are

About Anwaar Digital

Free, beautiful, professional apps — made with care for everyone.

The Developer

Anwaar Digital is an independent app studio founded by Malik Muhammad Anwaar — an app developer, website developer and cyber security learner from Pakistan. Every app on this website is personally designed, built and tested by him, with one standard in mind: professional quality that the world can be proud of.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: free, high-quality apps for everyone — especially for users in Pakistan. Too many great tools are locked behind payments or filled with aggressive ads. Anwaar Digital apps are fully free, with clean design and honest functionality — no hidden charges, no tricks.

What We Offer

  • 28 Android apps — Islamic apps (Islam 786, Hadith 786, Quran Teacher 786), tools (CV Maker, PDF Master, Image Resizer, Video Downloader PRO), utilities and more — all as direct APK downloads.
  • Games — real 3D racing, Ludo, Hill Climb and more, built to a genuine Play Store standard.
  • Windows software — desktop companions for our most-loved tools.
  • Cyber security guides — practical, beginner-friendly guides to staying safe online.
  • YouTube channels — educational and entertainment content, including AjoobaJahan and more, linked from the site.

Why Free?

We believe quality software should not be a luxury. Many users in Pakistan cannot afford paid apps or expensive subscriptions, yet they deserve the same professional experience as anyone else. Everything here is free — and will stay that way.

The Quality Bar

Every app must earn its place: beautiful interface, smooth performance, and real usefulness. Nothing is published until it is checked on real devices. If something breaks, it is fixed — not ignored. That is the deal with our users.

Our Promise

Every release is checked before it reaches you. If an app has a problem, report it via Contact or WhatsApp — genuine issues are fixed in updates. We respect your privacy (see our Privacy Policy) and keep our terms fair (see Terms of Service).

Where to Find Us

Besides this website, Anwaar Digital is active on YouTube, where educational and storytelling videos are published regularly. For direct contact, use WhatsApp or email malikanwaarapps@gmail.com. New apps and updates are announced on the website first, so check back often.
