How to Download APK Safely

Play Store ke bahar se APK download karna safe bhi ho sakta hai — bas ye 5 usool yaad rakhein...

Kabhi kabhi app Play Store par nahi milti, to APK download karna parta hai. Safe tareeqa ye hai:

  1. Sirf trusted website se download karein — jaise anwaardigital.github.io. Ajnabi links par click na karein.
  2. Permissions check karein — Agar torch app contacts mang rahi hai, to kuch ghalat hai!
  3. Play Protect on rakhein — Install ke waqt Google scan karta hai.
  4. Modded / cracked APKs se bachein — In mein aksar malware hota hai.
  5. App ke baad source verify karein — Developer ka naam aur website match honi chahiye.

Protect Your Phone: 7 Tips

Aapka phone hackers se kitna mehfooz hai? Ye 7 aasaan tips aapko safe rakhen gi...

  1. Screen lock lazmi — PIN ya fingerprint, pattern se behtar.
  2. Apps update rakhein — Updates mein security fixes hote hain.
  3. Public WiFi par banking na karein — Hackers data chura sakte hain.
  4. Unknown links na kholen — WhatsApp/SMS ke ajeeb links se bachein.
  5. App permissions review karein — Jo app jo permission mangti hai, soch kar dein.
  6. Backup lete rahein — Photos aur contacts ka cloud backup.
  7. SafeBox jaisi app use karein — Private photos/videos ko lock karein.
