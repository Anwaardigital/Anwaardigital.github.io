How to Download APK Safely

Downloading APKs outside the Play Store can be safe — just remember these 5 rules...

Sometimes an app is not on the Play Store, so you need to download the APK. Here is the safe way:

  1. Only download from a trusted website — like anwaardigital.com. Never click unknown links.
  2. Check permissions — If a torch app asks for your contacts, something is wrong!
  3. Keep Play Protect ON — Google scans the app during installation.
  4. Avoid modded / cracked APKs — They often contain malware.
  5. Verify the source after install — The developer name and website should match.

All Anwaar Digital APKs are virus-free and safe.

Protect Your Phone: 7 Tips

How safe is your phone from hackers? These 7 easy tips will keep you protected...

  1. Always use a screen lock — PIN or fingerprint is better than a pattern.
  2. Keep your apps updated — Updates include security fixes.
  3. Avoid banking on public WiFi — Hackers can steal your data.
  4. Don't open unknown links — Beware of strange links in WhatsApp/SMS.
  5. Review app permissions — Think before granting any permission an app asks for.
  6. Take regular backups — Cloud backup for your photos and contacts.
  7. Use an app like SafeBox — Lock your private photos and videos.

Hidden Android Features

Your phone can do a lot with these 6 hidden features — most people don't know about them...

  1. Split Screen — Run two apps at once (Recent apps → tap app icon → Split screen).
  2. App Pinning — Lock to one app when handing your phone to someone (Settings → Security).
  3. Notification History — See notifications you accidentally dismissed.
  4. One-Handed Mode — Use a big phone easily with one hand.
  5. Screen Recorder — Record your screen without any app (in Quick Settings).
