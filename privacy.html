Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what we collect and how it is used.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Anwaar Digital ("we", "our") operates the website anwaardigital.github.io and publishes free Android applications. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to the minimum needed to run our apps and services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information (Firebase Auth): if you log in to our website or apps with your email or Google account, we receive your name, email address and profile information. This is used only for sign-in, account features and support.
  • Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging): some apps request permission to send notifications. A device token may be stored to deliver them. You can disable notifications anytime in your device settings.
  • Data you enter in apps: information you type inside our apps (for example text in note apps or CV details) stays on your device unless the app clearly offers cloud backup. We do not sell or share it.
  • We do NOT collect your precise location, contacts, call logs or photos without your explicit in-app action.

2. APK Downloads — No Tracking

APK files on this website are offered as direct downloads. Downloading an app does not require an account, and we do not fingerprint or track your downloads for advertising purposes.

3. Cookies & Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics (GA4, measurement ID G-T6WVGJVD4J) to understand anonymous, aggregated usage statistics — for example, which pages are visited. Google Analytics may collect your IP address, device and browser information on Google's servers, processed under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not combine it with any personal information. You can opt out at any time with Google's official opt-out browser add-on.

Dark-mode preference and other basic preferences are stored in your browser's local storage — not third-party tracking cookies.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics (GA4): used on this website for anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (see section 3 above).
  • Google AdSense (planned): we intend to show ads via Google AdSense to keep the site free. When enabled, Google may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • Firebase (Google): used for authentication, database and push notifications in our apps. Data is processed under Google's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages: this website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log standard server information (such as IP addresses) for security.
  • Google Fonts: used to display the Poppins typeface. Google may collect usage data per its own policy.

5. Children's Privacy

Our apps are designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Parents who believe a child provided personal data may contact us to request deletion.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, or to ask anything about this policy, email us at malikanwaarapps@gmail.com. We aim to respond within a reasonable time.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including Firebase's built-in security rules. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected. Account data is retained while your account exists; data stored locally in apps remains on your device until you delete the app or clear its data. Upon a verified deletion request, we remove your personal data from our systems within a reasonable time.

9. International Transfers

Our third-party providers (such as Google Firebase) may process data on servers outside Pakistan. By using our apps and website, you consent to such processing in accordance with this policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of our website or apps after changes means you accept the updated policy.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please see our Contact page.
