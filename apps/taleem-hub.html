Taleem Hub is built for one purpose: to give every student a complete study toolkit in a single app. Version 2.0 packs 590 chapters of organized study notes covering the key subjects and classes students actually need, so you can revise a full chapter before exams without hunting through books.

Practice is where marks are made, and the app includes 1,265 multiple-choice questions for self-testing. Attempt them topic by topic, check your answers instantly, and repeat until the concept sticks. Taleem Hub gathers learning resources for Pakistani students in one place. For families who cannot afford expensive academies, Taleem Hub puts quality study support in every student's pocket — free.
