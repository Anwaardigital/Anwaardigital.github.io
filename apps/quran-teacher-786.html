Tips

Pro Tips

💡 Master the alphabet first

Do not rush to verses. Spend proper time on letter recognition — it makes everything after it ten times easier.

💡 Listen, then repeat aloud

Play the audio, listen carefully, then recite the same verse yourself. Your ear trains your tongue.

💡 Practice a little every day

Fifteen focused minutes daily beats three hours once a week. Consistency is the secret of Quranic learning.

About

About Quran Teacher 786

Finding a good Quran teacher should be simple. Quran Teacher 786 is a dedicated platform where students and parents can browse teacher profiles — qualifications, experience, teaching style and availability — and book classes directly through the app.

Teachers get their own side of the app: create a professional profile, list what you teach (Nazra, Hifz, Tajweed, translation), set your timings and fees, and receive booking requests. The built-in chat makes it easy to discuss schedules, fees and class details before committing.

The whole app works in both Urdu and English, so parents and students across Pakistan can use it comfortably. Whether you want to learn the Quran with Tajweed, help your child start Nazra, or offer your own teaching services, Quran Teacher 786 brings both sides together in one trusted place.

Quran Teacher 786 is designed for those learning to read the Holy Quran correctly — especially beginners, children and new Muslims. The app breaks learning into manageable steps: recognising Arabic letters, joining them into words, then reading verses with proper tajweed guidance. Audio recitation lets learners hear each verse before trying it themselves, which is the traditional and most effective way to learn. Progress at your own pace without pressure, repeating lessons as often as needed until reading feels natural.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Quran Teacher 786 free?

Downloading and browsing is free. Teachers set their own fees for classes, which you discuss and agree on directly with them.

Can I offer my own teaching services?

Yes. Register as a teacher, build your profile with your qualifications and timings, and start receiving booking requests.

Does it work offline?

You can view saved profiles offline, but browsing, chat and booking require an internet connection.

What Android version do I need?

The app works on Android 7.0 (Nougat) and above.

Is it safe?

Profiles are reviewed, and all communication happens inside the app's chat so you never have to share personal contact details until you're comfortable.

Is this suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. The app starts from the Arabic alphabet and builds up step by step.

Does it teach tajweed rules?

Yes. Pronunciation guidance is included so learners read correctly from the start.
