Pro Tips

Use a code you will remember but others cannot guess. Avoid birthdays and repeating numbers.

💡 Back up before hiding

Keep a copy of truly irreplaceable photos elsewhere too — SafeBox protects privacy, but no app replaces a backup.

If the app offers a decoy mode, set it up. It shows a fake empty vault if someone forces you to open the app.
