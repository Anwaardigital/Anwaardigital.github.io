About SafeBox

SafeBox protects the photos and videos you want to keep private. Move personal files into the vault and they disappear from your gallery, protected behind a PIN that only you know. Unlike cloud-based vaults, SafeBox stores everything locally on your phone — your private data never leaves your device, never gets uploaded, and can never be seen by anyone else. The app has a discreet design, quick import from your gallery, and a PIN recovery option through your Gmail so you never lose access permanently. It is simple enough for anyone to use, yet serious enough to trust with your most personal memories.
