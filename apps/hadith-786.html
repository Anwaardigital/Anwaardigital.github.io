About

About Hadith 786

Hadith 786 makes the blessed sayings of the Prophet ﷺ accessible to every Muslim. The app contains 2,000 ahadith selected from 6 major hadith books, covering faith, worship, character, family life, business ethics and daily conduct.

Every hadith is presented in three languages — Arabic, Urdu and English — so you can read the original text alongside translations you understand. Each narration includes its authenticity grading (Sahih, Hasan, Da'if), helping you know the status of what you're reading — a feature missing from many hadith apps.

A special highlight is the human-voice audio: listen to ahadith recited by a real human voice rather than robotic speech, perfect for learning while driving, working or resting. The library works offline, and you can bookmark favorites and share ahadith with family and friends.

Hadith 786 brings the sayings of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) to your phone in easy Urdu. The collection is organised by topic — prayer, character, family, business, daily life — so you can find relevant guidance in seconds. Each hadith is presented with its reference, and the Urdu translation makes the meaning clear for every reader. Whether you want a daily hadith for reflection or need a specific narration for study, the app's search and topic browsing make it effortless.
