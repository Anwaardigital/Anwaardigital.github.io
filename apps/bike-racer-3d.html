Tips

Pro Tips

💡 Brake before sharp turns

Slow down just before a tight corner, then accelerate out of it. Carrying too much speed into turns is the most common reason for crashing.

💡 Learn each track's rhythm

Replay the same track a few times to memorise where the sharp turns and boosts are. Familiarity beats raw speed.

💡 Keep your line smooth

Avoid sudden left-right movements. Gentle, steady steering keeps your speed up and your bike stable.
