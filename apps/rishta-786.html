About Rishta 786

Finding a suitable rishta is one of life's most important decisions, and it deserves a platform built on trust. Rishta 786 is a matrimonial app where every profile goes through admin approval before appearing — no fake or incomplete profiles cluttering your search.

Create a detailed profile with your age, education, profession, family background, city and partner preferences. Search and filter by the criteria that matter to your family — caste, sect, education, city and more. When you find a promising match, the secure in-app chat lets families talk safely without sharing phone numbers too early.

Privacy is central: you control what information is visible and to whom. Profiles are verified to reduce fake accounts, and the reporting system helps keep the community respectful. Rishta 786 is designed for serious marriage proposals — for individuals and families across Pakistan.
