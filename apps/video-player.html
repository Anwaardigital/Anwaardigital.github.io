About Video Player

Video Player plays virtually any video file you throw at it — MP4, MKV, AVI and more — in smooth, stutter-free HD. The interface is clean and distraction-free, with smart gestures: swipe on the sides of the screen to adjust brightness and volume, swipe sideways to seek. It supports subtitles in multiple formats, remembers where you stopped watching, and organises your videos into a tidy library automatically. Whether it is a downloaded lecture, a family video or your favourite drama episode, playback is fast, battery-friendly and beautiful.
