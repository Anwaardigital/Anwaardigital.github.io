Pro Tips

💡 Try filters before shooting

Preview filters live in the camera so you capture the mood you want, instead of fixing it afterwards.

💡 Use natural light when possible

Photos taken near a window or outdoors look dramatically better than indoor artificial light, whatever the filter.

💡 Do not overdo the effects

One filter plus light adjustments usually looks better than stacking many effects. Subtle edits win.
