About

About ChatBox

ChatBox is a clean, fast messaging app with the features you use every day. Send text messages, voice notes, photos and videos in one-to-one chats, or bring everyone together in group chats for family, friends, classmates or work teams.

Privacy features are built in: send view-once photos and videos that disappear after being seen, and use delete for everyone to remove a message you sent by mistake. Read receipts, typing indicators and online status keep conversations feeling live and natural.

The interface is simple and familiar — if you can use any modern messenger, you already know ChatBox. It's lightweight, quick to install, and works well even on slower connections. ChatBox v4.1 keeps your conversations flowing without clutter or complications.
