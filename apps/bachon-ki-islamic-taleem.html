About Bachon ki Islamic Taleem

Bachon ki Islamic Taleem helps children fall in love with their deen through listening and play. Every surah, dua, kalma and story is narrated in a warm human voice that children can repeat after — the app never uses artificial or robotic voices, so pronunciation learning feels natural and friendly. Parents get a completely safe space with no inappropriate content and no chat features. Children can memorise short surahs step by step, learn the six kalmas, hear stories of the Prophets and the seerat of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, and test what they learned with fun quizzes.

Key Features

Surahs with Human-Voice Audio

Short surahs are recited in a clear, slow human voice that children can repeat after. Verse-by-verse playback helps kids memorise at their own pace.

Six Kalmas with Translation

All six kalmas with Arabic text, easy Urdu meaning and transliteration, so children understand what they are saying as well as how to say it.

Masnoon Duas for Daily Life

Duas for waking up, eating, drinking, going to the mosque, sleeping and more — taught through friendly audio children enjoy repeating.

Prophet Stories for Kids

Beautifully narrated stories of the Prophets and the seerat of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, told in simple language that keeps young listeners engaged.

Fun Quizzes & Rewards

Short quizzes after lessons check what your child learned, with encouraging feedback that motivates them to keep learning more.

100% Kid-Safe, No Robotic Voices

No chat, no strangers, no inappropriate content — and every single audio in the app is recorded by a real human voice, never computer-generated.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is the app free?

Yes, Bachon ki Islamic Taleem is completely free for every family — no payments and no locked lessons.

Q: Is the audio really human?

Yes. Every recitation, dua and story in the app is narrated by a real human voice. The app does not use robotic or AI-generated voices anywhere.

Q: Is it safe for young children?

Absolutely. There is no chat, no external links, no social features and no inappropriate content — only Islamic learning material.

Q: Does it need the internet?

The app works offline after the lessons are downloaded once, so children can learn anywhere without internet.

Q: Which ages is it for?

The content is designed for children roughly between 4 and 12 years old, but younger kids can listen along with parents too.
