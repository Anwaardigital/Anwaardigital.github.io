About Document Scanner

Document Scanner replaces the flatbed scanner with your phone. Point the camera at any document — an ID card, a certificate, a receipt, handwritten notes — and the app captures it with autofocus lock for maximum sharpness. Adjust the corners to crop precisely, pick the B&W document filter for that clean scanned look, and save or share a high-resolution PDF. Students scanning assignments, job seekers digitising certificates, shopkeepers archiving receipts: everyone gets professional scans without any hardware.

Every scan is enhanced on-device. The adaptive black-and-white filter handles shadows and uneven lighting, so text stays readable even from imperfect photos. Multi-page documents are supported — keep adding pages, then export everything as a single PDF. All processing happens on your phone; your documents are never uploaded anywhere.

Pro Tips

💡 For sharpest scans

Take the photo with your phone's camera app, then use Import from Gallery — this gives the cleanest source image.

💡 Hold steady

When using the in-app camera, wait for the "Focusing, hold steady..." message before the shot is taken.

💡 Good lighting matters

Natural daylight gives the best results. Avoid harsh shadows across the document.
