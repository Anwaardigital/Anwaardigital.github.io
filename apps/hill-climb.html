About

About Hill Climb

Hill Climb is all about one thing: getting your vehicle up the mountain. With simple two-pedal controls — gas and brake — you'll bounce over rugged terrain, launch off ramps, and perform flips and airtime stunts across endless procedurally generated hills.

Coins collected on your climbs buy upgrades: a stronger engine, better tires, improved suspension and more fuel. Each upgrade lets you climb further and tackle steeper, wilder stages — from countryside hills to moon-like landscapes with low gravity.

The physics-based gameplay is easy to learn but hard to master: too much gas on a steep slope and you'll flip backward; too little and you'll stall. Chase high scores, unlock new vehicles and stages, and see how far up the mountain you can go. Hill Climb v3.4 is pure arcade fun for all ages.
