Pro Tips

💡 Trim before you arrange

Cut each clip to exactly the part you want first, then arrange them on the timeline. Editing is much faster this way.

💡 Keep text short and readable

Use brief captions in a large font. Viewers on phones will thank you.

💡 Export a draft and rewatch

Before sharing, export once and watch the full video. You will spot small mistakes that are easy to fix.
