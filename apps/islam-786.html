About Islam 786

Islam 786 was built to put the daily essentials of a Muslim's life in one place. Whether you want to check the exact namaz time for your city, read the Holy Quran with Urdu translation, find a dua for a special occasion, locate the Qibla direction while travelling, or count your daily zikr on the digital tasbeeh — Islam 786 does it all without any subscription or cost. The interface is clean and simple, so even elderly family members can use it comfortably. Everything important works offline after the first setup, and prayer reminders keep you on time wherever you are.

Key Features

Accurate Prayer Times & Azan Alerts

See all five daily namaz times calculated for your city, with optional azan notifications so you never miss a prayer. Times adjust to your location and include Sehri and Iftar timings during Ramadan.

Holy Quran with Urdu Translation

Read the complete Quran with clear Arabic text and Urdu translation. Navigate by surah or para, bookmark your favourite ayahs, and continue reading exactly where you left off.

Daily Duas Collection

A large collection of authentic duas for everyday situations — waking up, eating, travelling, entering the home, sleeping, and special occasions — each with Arabic text, Urdu meaning and transliteration.

Qibla Compass

Find the exact Qibla direction from anywhere in the world with the built-in compass. Perfect for travellers who need to pray on the go.

Digital Tasbeeh Counter

Count your daily zikr with the beautiful digital tasbeeh. Set a target (e.g. 100 or 500), get a gentle vibration when you reach it, and track your daily counts.

Islamic Calendar & Hijri Date

Always know the current Hijri date at a glance, with important Islamic days and nights marked throughout the year.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is Islam 786 free?

Yes, Islam 786 is completely free to download and use. There are no hidden charges, no subscriptions and no premium locks — all features are open for everyone.

Q: Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first setup, prayer times, the Quran, duas and the tasbeeh all work without an internet connection. Only the initial location setup needs connectivity.

Q: Are the prayer times accurate for my city?

The app calculates prayer times from your location using standard calculation methods. If your local mosque follows a slightly different schedule, you can fine-tune the offsets in settings.

Q: What Android version do I need?

Islam 786 works on Android 7.0 (Nougat) and above, which covers virtually all phones in use today.
