About

About Sehat 786

Good health information should be available to everyone, in the language they understand. Sehat 786 collects 2,878 health topics organized into 36 categories — covering common illnesses, home remedies, traditional totkay, diet and nutrition, fitness, women's and children's health, and preventive care.

Each topic is written in clear, practical language in Urdu and English. You can look up symptoms and first-aid guidance, learn which home remedies are traditionally used for everyday problems like cold, cough, stomach issues and headaches, and get diet advice suited to Pakistani food habits.

The app also covers lifestyle topics: exercise routines, weight management, sleep, stress, and healthy habits for the whole family. The entire library works offline, so health guidance is available even when there's no internet. Note: the app is for general health information and does not replace a doctor's advice.

Sehat 786 is a health companion in Urdu for the whole family. It covers everyday health topics — common illnesses, first aid, nutrition, mother-and-child care, and healthy daily habits — explained in simple language anyone can understand. The guidance is practical and Pakistan-relevant, from dealing with seasonal flu to knowing when a symptom needs a doctor. It is not a replacement for medical professionals, but a trustworthy first reference that helps you make better health decisions at home.
