Tips

Pro Tips

💡 Do not rush all tokens out

Bringing every token out early makes you a target. Keep one or two safe while you advance the leaders.

💡 Use safe squares wisely

Park tokens on starred safe squares when opponents are close behind — patience wins games.

💡 Watch opponents' positions

Before moving, glance at where rivals are. Sometimes the safe move beats the aggressive one.

About

About Ludo

The timeless game of Ludo is back on your phone. Ludo recreates the classic board game with bright, colorful graphics, smooth dice animations and the satisfying feeling of knocking an opponent's token back to start.

Play local multiplayer with family and friends on a single phone — just like the real board — or take on computer opponents of different difficulty levels. The classic rules are all here: roll a six to bring a token out, race around the board, capture rivals and get all four tokens home first.

With quick matches, clean design and simple tap controls, Ludo v2.1 is perfect for family gatherings, long journeys and evening fun. No complicated setup — just open the app and roll the dice.

Ludo on your phone captures everything people love about the board game: the tension of a six, the joy of capturing a rival, the race to bring all four tokens home.
