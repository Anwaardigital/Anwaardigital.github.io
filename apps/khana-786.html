About

About Khana 786

Pakistani and desi cooking deserves its own recipe app. Khana 786 brings 903 recipes together — from everyday daal-sabzi and roti to special-occasion biryani, nihari, haleem and a full range of meethay (desserts).

Recipes are organized into 24 categories — nashta, salan, BBQ, snacks, drinks, pickles, diet food and more — so you always know where to look. Each recipe lists ingredients and step-by-step instructions in both Urdu and English, written the way home cooks actually talk.

Beyond recipes, the app includes 160 practical kitchen tips: how to keep spices fresh, fix an over-salted salan, store atta, tenderize meat without a pressure cooker, and dozens of other tricks that make daily cooking easier. Everything works offline, so you can follow a recipe in the kitchen even with no signal.
