About

About Karobar 786

Starting and running a business in Pakistan comes with its own realities — and most business advice online is written for other countries. Karobar 786 changes that with 805 business articles written in simple Urdu for Pakistani entrepreneurs.

The articles cover the full journey: choosing a business idea, small investment businesses, shop and wholesale trade, online selling, marketing on a budget, dealing with customers, managing cash flow, hiring staff, and scaling up. Whether you run a kiryana store, a clothing shop, a food business or an online store, there is guidance matched to your situation.

Alongside the articles, the app includes 20 practical business tools — calculators and helpers for profit margins, pricing, monthly expenses, break-even analysis and more — so you can plan with real numbers, not guesses. Everything works offline, so you can read and calculate anywhere.
